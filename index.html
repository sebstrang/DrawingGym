Built around free resources: Drawabox (drawabox.com) for spatial reasoning & ink mark-making, Love Life Drawing (lovelifedrawing.com) for figure, gesture & portrait, and Ctrl+Paint (ctrlpaint.com) for digital — with the occasional free Proko YouTube video where it's the clearest explanation. Everything linked is free. A 24-week course in four stages, six sessions a week. Tools: a fineliner pen and cheap paper to start; a tablet from Stage 2. Named for Sebastiano Ricci (1659–1734), a Venetian painter whose brushwork was quick and sure.
